[MicroZig](https://microzig.tech/) is a framework for building firmware for microcontrollers (MCU). The
purpose is to provide convenience in the form of generated linkerscripts, MMIO
access code, and more. But not so far that the system is abstracted away. We are
here to program the hardware after all.

This session will cover some of the lore behind MicroZig, with some demos
showing a barebones system on a breadboard, and a wearable conference badge.
