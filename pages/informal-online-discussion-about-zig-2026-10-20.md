We had a cosy informal online discussion about Zig.

Some of the topics that were mentioned

* [Marionette](https://github.com/sb2bg/marionette)  Deterministic I/O and simulation testing for Zig by [Sullivan Bognar](https://sbognar.com/)
* [Tiger Beetle](https://tigerbeetle.com/) Financial Transactions Database.
* [Jepsen](https://jepsen.io/), a tool to improve the safety of distributed databases, queues, consensus systems, etc.
* [Software You Can Love](https://softwareyoucan.love/) conferences.
* [Zig Days](https://zig.day/) - Zig hackathons, conferences.
* [Ziggit](https://ziggit.dev/) - Forum of the Zig community.
* [Ziglings](https://codeberg.org/ziglings/exercises/) exercises to learn Zig.

* [Zig Maven online events](https://live.code-maven.com/zig)
